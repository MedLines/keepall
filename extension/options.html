Link saving

Right-click a link on a website and choose Save to Keepall. The link goes straight to Unsorted, without opening it first. Already-saved links keep their current collection.

Saving links does not need the extra website permissions used for image downloads.

The same menu action works for images: right-click an image to save the image itself, or right-click a text link to save its destination.

Selected text

Highlight text on a page, right-click, and choose Save to Keepall. The text is saved as a note alongside the page's link, so you can return to its source.

If the page is already in your library, the text is added below its existing note. Your collection, tags, and earlier writing stay in place. Saving the same passage again won't add another copy.

Image saving

1. Right-click an image

Choose Save to Keepall from the menu. New images go straight to Unsorted in your Keepall library, ready for you to organize later.

2. Allow access to that website

The first time you save an image from a new website, Chrome asks for permission. Choose Allow to save the image. Chrome remembers your choice, so future saves from that same website won't need another permission prompt.

What this means for your privacy

Chrome says "read and change your data" because this permission allows broad website access, including downloading images. Keepall uses it to save the image you choose. It does not read or change your account data, collect passwords or browsing history, or upload your saved images to our servers.

Your selected image and its source link are saved locally in Keepall, on your device. New images go to Unsorted.

You can change website access in Chrome's extension settings. Your saved items stay in your library.

Change or remove website access

  1. Open Manage access in Chrome below. Chrome opens Keepall Capture's extension details.
  2. Under Site access, choose On specific sites instead of On all sites.
  3. Keep https://www.keepall.app in the allowed sites so the extension can save to your library. For local testing, keep your localhost library address allowed too.
  4. Remove any other website from Allowed sites using its menu. You can add websites here when you want to allow them again.

These controls change Chrome's website permissions. They do not delete saved items or disable saving links. Choosing to save an image or allowing a website again can grant access for that save; removing access does not guarantee Chrome will repeat an earlier permission prompt.
